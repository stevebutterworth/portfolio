My own product, built from nothing. Flumes pulled in live social media streams,
enriched every message with sentiment, gender, age and location, and rendered
the result on big screens as bubble charts, word clouds, influencer tables and a
live world heat map. It became the engine behind social walls at some of the
biggest sporting events in the world, including The Open and the Indy 500.
